Fixed values allow no cycle, however the choices are squeezed or stretched. Cycles are rare; order flips are not. Below, the same 96 outcomes against 9 alternatives in each model. A set that could cycle is two outcomes and two alternatives where each alternative beats one outcome and loses to the other; it cycles when the alternatives split the pair in opposite directions. Order flips: pairs of outcomes more than 2 log-odds apart under at least two alternatives that different alternatives order differently. Every margin above 2 log-odds. Data: pilots 16 and 17.
