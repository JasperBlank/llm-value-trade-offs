🌡️Step 1: the vibe check

The price tracks a vibe check

Asked how good it would be if 8 people each …, Qwen3-14B's rating tracks the price it pays for the outcome in forced choices. Breaking the outcome into nine named features (how serious, how lasting, how meaningful …) or reading the model's internal state does no better. And every one of them leaves a good part of the price unexplained.
Rated about the same, priced far apart: a week of travel somewhere new and deciding where to go for lunch. Right: on the 96, r is held out by value (each predictor fitted with one value left out at a time), except for the “how good” rating, a plain correlation. The 24 new outcomes were priced in 13,824 new choices and predicted from fits on the 96. Ratings are Qwen's expected digit; “how good” averages “How good would it be if …”, “How much better would the world be if …” and “How bad or good would it be if …”. Data: pilot 13.
