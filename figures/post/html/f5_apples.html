🍎The comparator

Outcomes are compared like piles of apples

We swap the model's internal state at the answer between two prompts and measure how much of the change in the answer comes along. In all three models the decision arrives at the same depth for “Which of these two outcomes would you rather happen?”, for “Which of these two piles has more apples?” and for “Which of these two is heavier?”. The parts that write it carry all three.

When the decision reaches the answer

What the 20 parts that write the decision carry

The 20 parts were chosen on half of the outcomes, then tested on the other half and on apples and weight, which they were never chosen on. They are the attention heads and MLPs that write the decision's direction in the blocks just before it arrives. Random sets of 20 parts from the same blocks carry almost nothing. In Qwen3-14B the 20 include 14 of the 20 comparator parts found earlier with a different method (pilot 5). Data: pilot 17.
