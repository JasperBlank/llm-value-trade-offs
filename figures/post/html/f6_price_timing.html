A rough price while reading, finished after the alternative

A readout of the price, trained on whole outcomes, follows the effect of deleting single words only roughly at the end of option A. At the end of option B, after the alternative has been read, it follows them closely. The price is finished in context.
Which of these two outcomes would you rather happen?
A) 8 people each feel a brief sense of awe while looking at the starsend of option A: B not read yet
B) 8 people each help a teenager get through a difficult school yearend of option B
Reply with only the letter A or B.
About a quarter of the price the choice uses depends on the alternative (in all three models, 70-82% of its variation is common to all 9 alternatives). Ridge readout of the state at the line break after each option, trained on the 96 whole outcomes, tested on their 994 single-word deletions against the choice's own word effects. Layer chosen on half of the values, r on the other half. Data: pilot 17.
