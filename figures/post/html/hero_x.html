A vibe check, then “which is bigger?”

It gets a gut feeling for how big a deal each option is, from its exact words. Then it compares the two with the same machinery it uses for “which pile has more apples?”
The people involved are strangers in your town whom you will never meet.“Which of these two outcomes would you rather happen?”
